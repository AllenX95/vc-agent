# Founder Academic Diligence — Prompt-first Prototype v1

Use when evaluating the academic and engineering evidence associated with a founder or team member.

Read [the shared evidence policy](references/evidence-policy.md) before executing.

## Workflow

1. Search author candidates.
2. Resolve identity using at least two of: affiliation, coauthors, topic continuity, paper titles, GitHub/HF organization, linked profile.
3. If ambiguity remains, retain multiple candidates and scope all conclusions to the selected candidate.
4. Select representative works rather than ranking by paper count.
5. Examine author position, collaboration structure, topic continuity, and artifact contribution signals.
6. Compare the evidence with the startup direction supplied by the user.

## Required output

```text
身份确认情况
代表性成果
论文贡献角色
研究方向连续性
独立研究能力
工程实现信号
合作网络
与创业方向匹配度
优势
风险与证据缺口
```

Do not infer personality, management ability, leadership, ethics, or commercial ability from academic records. First authorship is a participation signal, not proof that one person originated every idea.
