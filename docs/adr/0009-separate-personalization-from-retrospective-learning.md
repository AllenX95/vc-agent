# Separate Personalization From Evidence-Backed Investment Learning

Accepted: the product uses two complementary learning loops. Personalization captures and recalls the sole User's confirmed preferences, corrections, and judgment patterns, while Investment Retrospective compares earlier judgments and their original rationale with later evidence or outcomes before proposing a user-approved Judgment Revision. This prevents repetition or preference from being treated as proof, at the cost of preserving judgment provenance and revisiting projects over time.
