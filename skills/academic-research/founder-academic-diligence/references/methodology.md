# Founder Academic Diligence Methodology

## Identity

Require two or more matching signals. If ambiguity remains, define the candidate author record used and state that the conclusion is conditional.

## Contribution

Prefer representative work over counts. Consider author order, corresponding authorship, consistency with the person's research history, public code contributions, and whether the startup direction follows their demonstrated work.

## Assessment dimensions

- Research capability
- Originality and independent direction
- Sustained output
- Engineering evidence
- Collaboration network
- Fit with the startup's technical thesis

Do not reduce the assessment to h-index or citation totals.
