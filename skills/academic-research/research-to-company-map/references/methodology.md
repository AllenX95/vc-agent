# Research-To-Company Mapping Methodology

## Strong signals

- Paper, repository, model card, and organization profile explicitly cross-link.
- The organization name and commercial domain are stated in an original asset.
- Multiple paper authors publicly maintain the same organization assets.

## Medium signals

- Several authors join one organization and continue the same technical route.
- Organization assets consistently extend the seed work.

## Weak signals

- Similar names, forks, follows, isolated contributions, or thematic similarity.

Weak signals cannot establish a company relationship. Report them only as leads requiring confirmation from company websites, employment sources, corporate records, or financing data.
