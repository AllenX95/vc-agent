# Claim Verification Methodology

For each claim, identify:

1. The entity or system being claimed.
2. The exact capability, scale, benchmark, or comparison.
3. The test conditions, date, model size, data, hardware, and metric.
4. Whether the evidence is a paper, author statement, public artifact, or independent result.
5. The strongest counterexample or narrower interpretation.

For superlatives such as first, leading, or SOTA, report the search scope and date. Rewrite unsupported marketing language into the narrowest statement supported by evidence.
