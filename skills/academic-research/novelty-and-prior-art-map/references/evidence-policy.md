# Academic Evidence Policy

- Cite every externally verifiable fact with an Evidence ID from `academic_research`.
- Label statements as sourced fact, author claim, inference, or VC judgment.
- Prefer paper text and original artifacts over structured indexes.
- Treat OpenAlex as an index, arXiv as a preprint source, GitHub as engineering evidence, and Hugging Face Cards as publisher claims.
- Never equate arXiv with peer review, stars or downloads with adoption, repository existence with completeness, citations with commercial value, or a shared name with identity.
- Use `verified`, `broadly supported`, `partially supported`, `conflicting evidence`, `unverifiable`, or `refuted`; do not invent numeric confidence.
- Preserve source URL, retrieval time, and material limitations.
