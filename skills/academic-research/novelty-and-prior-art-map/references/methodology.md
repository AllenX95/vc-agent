# Novelty Mapping Methodology

Represent each work with title, year, route, identifiers, and evidence IDs.

Use only these relationship classes:

- foundational
- direct predecessor
- contemporary parallel work
- alternative route
- successor or improvement
- engineering implementation
- third-party reproduction

For each edge, record why the relationship exists and whether confidence is high, medium, or low. Compare the seed against its closest direct predecessor at the level of mechanism, data, compute, evaluation, and released artifacts.
