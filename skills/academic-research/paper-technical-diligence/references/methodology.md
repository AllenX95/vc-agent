# Paper Diligence Methodology

## Inspect

- Problem definition and claimed improvement
- Core mechanism and assumptions
- Training data, compute, parameters, hardware, and evaluation protocol
- Benchmark splits, metrics, baselines, ablations, uncertainty, and negative results
- Code, checkpoints, datasets, demos, licenses, maintenance, and reproducibility

## Judge

- Novelty: new mechanism, meaningful composition, or implementation refinement
- Experimental validity: whether evidence supports the breadth of the claim
- Scaling: demonstrated range versus extrapolation
- Product relevance: performance, reliability, cost, integration, and data constraints

Do not average these dimensions into a single score.
